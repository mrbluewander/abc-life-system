# Longevity Research Summary

**Summary for Master ABC:**

Based on the latest research in 2026, here is a concise summary of the benefits of NMN, Resveratrol, and Spermidine supplements:

1. **NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide):** Ranked high in clinical evidence for longevity benefits, with studies showing potential for improving cellular energy metabolism and reducing age-related decline.
2. **Resveratrol:** Popular for its anti-aging properties, but human evidence remains limited. Some studies suggest benefits for cardiovascular health and potential anti-cancer effects.
3. **Spermidine:** Shows promise for promoting cellular autophagy, which can help remove damaged cells and proteins, potentially leading to increased longevity.
